Simple 3-step protocol

A bounty with a verifiable finish line.

The funder writes the brief, the hunter links the work, and GenLayer reads both sides of the evidence before funds move.

01

Lock the brief

Post a title, plain-language spec, two source URLs, deadline, and GEN reward.

02

Submit the proof

03

Resolve the work

Anyone can call resolve. Accepted work pays the hunter; rejected work keeps funds with the funder.

Common questions

A clearer way to commission work.

Why are two URLs required?

Validators compare evidence from two distinct hosts so a single page is not the only source of truth.

What does resolve do?

It asks GenLayer validators to read the submitted work against the bounty spec and settle the reserved funds.

Can a funder cancel?

An open bounty can be cancelled by its funder, returning the reserved amount according to the contract rules.
